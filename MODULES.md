# Σ∞ module registry

The canonical module registry of the **Polycosmic Hypercomplex Brain · Σ∞**
architecture (Jesús Vilela Jato, Madrid, July 2026), imported here from the
thesis Appendix A so the registry lives under version control and the claim
ledger rather than only inside a document.

> **Thesis position, carried over unchanged.** This is a mathematical and
> computational research architecture. It is not an established theory of
> neuroscience, consciousness, quantum cognition, or general intelligence.
> Hypercomplex algebra is used primarily as an **observability and operator
> language**. Semantic modules become scientific claims only through typed
> operators, controlled measurement, or proof.
>
> The eight mind qualities are **engineering coordinates and coupling laws,
> never evidence of sentience**.

## Core law

> **RESONATE ≠ ABSORB.** A successful translation makes a source readable
> without exhausting it. A successful global section coordinates local worlds
> without erasing their typed remainder.

This is the same discipline `STATUS.md` enforces mechanically: a structural
`PASS` makes a source readable to the validator without exhausting what the
source claims, and `NOT_TESTED` is the typed remainder the validator refuses to
absorb.
