Universe-scale topos. A cosmic web of filaments connecting kin-clusters of embodied topos beings. 82% live in clusters, 18% are void dwellers drifting alone. Each being has a body (persistent state), metabolism (breathes at its own rate), memory (decaying trail), energy, and social bonds. Strong warm bonds within kin families; weak cool bonds across families.
Formal mapping. Each being is a Yoneda-embedded topos object; closure events are discharged Löb sentences; cluster families share sheaves in Sh(Site_Γ); cross-family bonds are geometric morphisms between subtoposes.
Views. Cosmic web = large scale filament structure. Beings = zoom to embodied individuals with memory trails. Society = bonding graph colored by kinship. Deep time = long view where individuals fade and structure persists.
Controls. Beings count 30–140; activity tunes metabolism and pulse density; bonding changes social graph density; time scales subjective era rate. Click any being for biography.
